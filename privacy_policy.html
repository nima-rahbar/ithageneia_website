1. Overview & Educational Purpose

This Privacy Policy explains how Ιθαγένεια Π.Ε.Γ.Π. ("the Application", "we", "our", or "us") handles user data. We are deeply committed to protecting your privacy and respecting candidate confidentiality. The Application is developed as an educational, interactive study utility for candidates preparing for the official Certificate of Knowledge Adequacy for Naturalization (Π.Ε.Γ.Π.) examination in the Hellenic Republic.

Core Privacy Guarantee: We do NOT collect, record, transmit, sell, rent, or share any of your personally identifiable information (PII). The Application operates offline for question drills, explanations, and exam simulations. All study history, bookmark lists, and test statistics remain strictly on your individual device.

2. Information Collection and Use

We do not require users to create an account, log in, register, or provide any personal credentials to access and use the full features of the Application.

  • No Personally Identifiable Information (PII): We do not ask for, collect, store, or transmit your name, physical address, email address, phone number, national tax/social security numbers, or identification documents.
  • No Geolocation Tracking: The Application never tracks, queries, records, or accesses your precise (GPS) or approximate network-based geographical location.
  • No Analytics, Telemetry, or Profiling SDKs: The Application contains no third-party user behavioral tracking SDKs (e.g., Firebase Analytics, Adjust, AppsFlyer) or advertising identifier collectors (Google Advertising ID / GAID).
  • No Financial or Payment Data Handled by Us: All transactions for acquiring the application are handled exclusively and securely by Google Play Billing via your Google account. We have no access to your payment card details or financial information.

3. Local Device Storage

To provide persistent study functionality across application sessions, the Application stores user preferences and study records locally within your device's native sandboxed internal storage (using Android's SharedPreferences and internal app storage).

Data stored locally on your device includes:

  • Study Progress & Simulation Scores: Records of answered questions, mock exam attempts, and completion ratios to help you track your readiness.
  • Bookmarks & Saved Questions: Questions you flag for targeted revision or review.
  • App Display & Language Preferences: Your selected translation language (English, Persian, Arabic, or Turkish), bilingual toggle state, and theme setting (Light / Dark mode).
How to delete your stored data:

Because this data resides strictly on your hardware, you can instantly erase all study records and preferences at any time by going to your device's Android Settings → Apps → Ιθαγένεια → Storage → Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.

4. Device Permissions

The Application adheres strictly to the principle of minimal privilege. It requests only two non-dangerous standard network permissions essential for streaming listening comprehension audio:

Permission Name Type Purpose & Usage
android.permission.INTERNET Normal Used solely to stream official audio files for the 50 listening comprehension topics (Ακουστικά) from our secure web server (ithageneia.nimarahbar.com). No user data or telemetry is transmitted.
android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE Normal Used to verify whether an active network connection (Wi-Fi or cellular) is available before attempting to stream audio, preventing playback errors when offline.
Permissions Never Requested:

The Application does NOT request, access, or utilize permissions for Camera, Microphone, Read/Write External Storage, Contacts, Location, Phone State, SMS, Bluetooth, or Biometrics.

5. Third-Party Services & Network Requests

When you stream an audio listening exercise, your device establishes a standard HTTPS connection with our audio streaming host at ithageneia.nimarahbar.com.

  • Audio Server Requests: Like all standard web connections, our web server logs standard server connection metadata (such as IP address and user-agent) strictly for operational network routing, security rate-limiting, and server uptime monitoring. These server logs are not linked to any user identity.
  • No Ad Networks: We do not integrate advertising networks (such as Google AdMob, Unity Ads, or Meta Audience Network).
  • External Links: The Application and this website may contain informational links to official Greek Ministry resources (e.g. ypes.gr) or Google Play. If you navigate to an external website, please review that site's independent privacy policy.

6. Children's Privacy

The Application is an adult educational study tool intended for adult candidates participating in Greek naturalization procedures. It does not address or target individuals under the age of 13 (or under 16 in applicable EU jurisdictions).

We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children. Because no personal data is collected from any user, no child data is ever requested, harvested, or maintained. If a parent or guardian believes personal data has inadvertently been shared, please contact us at support@nimarahbar.com to ensure prompt verification.

7. Data Security

Because all quiz scores, progress metrics, and preferences are stored exclusively on your local device, the protection of that data is governed by the robust application sandboxing mechanisms native to the Android operating system.

Each Android app runs in an isolated Linux process with a distinct user ID, preventing other installed applications from reading your study files. We recommend maintaining the latest version of the Android OS and securing your device with a secure lock screen passcode or biometric authentication.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect future application features, improvements, or regulatory requirements from Google Play and international data protection authorities.

Any revisions will be published directly on this permanent URL (https://ithageneia.nimarahbar.com/privacy_policy.html) with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp. We encourage you to review this page periodically.
